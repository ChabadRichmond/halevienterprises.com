Strategic Investment. Enduring Value.

About Our Firm

Halevi Enterprises is a diversified private investment platform that deploys significant capital across structured credit, public securities, technology ventures, healthcare, and real estate. From our headquarters in New York, we manage a broad portfolio of assets spanning multiple sectors, geographies, and investment stages—maintaining the scale and infrastructure to execute complex, high-value transactions with the speed and discretion that institutional counterparties require.

Our Platform

We have spent decades building a capital base and institutional infrastructure that allows us to deploy hundreds of millions across our investment verticals each year—while maintaining the agility and decisiveness of a principal-led firm.

What We Do

Investment Capabilities

01

Private Credit & Structured Lending

Our flagship capability. We originate large-scale, bespoke credit instruments—including senior secured notes, structured promissory facilities, and multi-tranche debt arrangements—providing institutional-grade capital solutions to principals and operating companies across sectors. Our lending platform combines deep structuring expertise with the balance sheet capacity to underwrite significant commitments on a bilateral basis, without syndication or external capital dependency. We close with certainty and move with speed.

02

Public Markets & Active Securities

We manage a substantial portfolio across global equities, derivatives, and listed instruments, employing sophisticated strategies that span fundamental long-term positioning and tactical opportunistic trades. Our public markets desk operates as a core pillar of the platform—not a side allocation—generating returns and providing real-time market intelligence that informs our private investment decisions across all verticals.

03

Technology & Growth Capital

We deploy growth capital into select technology companies—with particular depth in fintech, banking infrastructure, payments, and enterprise software—where our investment can serve as a catalyst for the next stage of scale. We take concentrated positions and work closely with founding teams, bringing not only capital but access to our network of financial institutions, operating partners, and strategic advisors.

04

Healthcare & Essential Real Assets

Through both direct investments and publicly traded instruments, we maintain significant exposure to healthcare real estate, skilled nursing and rehabilitation facilities, assisted living communities, and other essential-service properties across the United States. Our real assets portfolio encompasses holdings across multiple states and is anchored in sectors with enduring demographic demand and recession-resistant characteristics.

05

Real Estate & Financial Institutions

We hold strategic positions in commercial real estate and in specialized banking and lending institutions that serve the broader real estate ecosystem. This dual approach—owning both the assets and the financial infrastructure that supports them—gives us visibility across the full real estate capital stack and the ability to identify opportunities at every level of the value chain.

Global Reach

Our capital moves across borders as fluidly as it moves across asset classes. We maintain deep relationships with leading counsel, structuring advisors, and institutional partners in multiple jurisdictions—enabling us to execute complex international transactions at scale.

Our Approach

How We Operate

Scale Without Bureaucracy

We combine the capital base and institutional infrastructure of a large platform with the speed and decisiveness of a principal-led firm. Our investment committee operates with a short chain of command, enabling us to commit significant capital on compressed timelines when the opportunity warrants it.

Certainty of Capital

Our counterparties choose us because our commitments are backed by substantial, readily deployable resources. When we sign a term sheet, the capital is available. This reliability gives us preferential access to transactions that require discretion, speed, and the assurance that the close will happen.

Institutional Rigor

Every transaction is underwritten to institutional standards—comprehensive legal structuring, robust downside protections, and meticulous due diligence. We engage top-tier counsel across every jurisdiction we operate in and maintain the compliance infrastructure appropriate to the scale and complexity of our activities.

Permanent Capital Advantage

Unlike fund-cycle-dependent firms, we deploy permanent capital with no artificial time horizons. This structural advantage allows us to hold positions through market cycles, structure patient financing arrangements, and build lasting relationships with counterparties who value stability and long-term commitment over transactional opportunism.
